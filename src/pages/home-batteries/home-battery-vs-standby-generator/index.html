Vs · Batteries and generators

A battery stores a tank of electricity. A standby generator makes a new tank from gas.

That is the whole argument. Everything else — noise, permits, 240 V, winter storms — follows from it.

Buy the battery path when

  • Outages are hours to a couple of days and you can shed HVAC.
  • You need indoor-safe electronics backup as the core job.
  • You might roll the kit to a cabin or a different house later.

Buy standby when

  • The house must behave like the grid for days, including heat pumps.
  • You will not babysit recharge math during an ice storm.
  • You have the install budget in generator cost.

Hybrid answers exist (battery + small gas, or battery + portable panels). They still obey kWh and surge. Rooftop-tied Powerwall-class systems are a third category we will not sneak in.
