Vs · Jackery, Bluetti, Anker SOLIX

These three leapfrog each other. Buy the inverter and the expansion story, not the fan color.

Jackery, Bluetti, and Anker SOLIX all sell LiFePO4 stations in the $200+ ticket range this site features. Year to year they copy capacity and watt claims. The differences that still matter are voltage, extra batteries, charge-port limits, and how you will actually live with the app.

Choose Jackery when

  • You want the most recognizable cart and a simple Explorer/SolarSaga pair.
  • You are staying on 120 V essentials and maybe extra Plus batteries.

Choose Bluetti when

  • You care about stacking kWh in a garage tower more than brand familiarity.
  • You will read input limits instead of daisy-chaining random panels.

Choose Anker SOLIX when the house hook and 240 V are the point, or you already live in Anker’s charger ecosystem and want one support channel. Then also read expandable home batteries.

Honest caveats

Any of the three can be the “best” SKU the month you buy. Firmware, warranty length, and which extra battery is in stock change. We will not publish fake lab scores. EcoFlow is the usual fourth name; we leave it out of featured CTAs because a 7-day cookie is a bad match for this category.
