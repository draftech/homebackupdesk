Contact

Write the desk. Do not write the manufacturers through us.

The Home Backup is a small editorial site. Email is the whole inbox. We do not run a ticket system, live chat, or a warranty desk for Jackery, BLUETTI, Anker SOLIX, or EcoFlow.

thehomebackup.com · editorial@thehomebackup.com

Send mail to editorial@thehomebackup.com. That address is the working brand mailbox. Until the domain is live, treat it as the public contact we will use — do not invent a second “support@” alias from this repository.

What we will answer

  • Corrections. A dated spec, street price, or photograph that no longer matches the manufacturer page. Include the URL on this site and the manufacturer page you checked.
  • Affiliate programs. Impact, Awin, or a brand partnership manager with a real tracking URL. We paste IDs into src/affiliates.json and netlify.toml. We do not invent click IDs in email either.
  • Press and reprints. Short quotes are fine with a link back. Do not scrape the catalog into a “top 50 solar generators” roundup.
  • Scope questions. If you want rooftop solar, Tesla Powerwall, or an installer lead, we will point you off the site. That is the product.

What we will not answer

  • Warranty claims, firmware bugs, or “why won’t my fridge start.” Write the brand. Start at Jackery, BLUETTI, Anker SOLIX, or EcoFlow.
  • Permit, gas-line, or transfer-switch advice for a specific address. Hire an electrician. Read the generator buying guide for the order of operations, not a stamp of approval.
  • Coupon codes we have not published. We do not keep a secret stash in this inbox.
  • Guest posts about “energy independence,” crypto miners, or roof-mount kits.

How to file a spec correction

Named SKUs on this site are locked to manufacturer pages dated in src/products.json (currently August 26, 2026). If Jackery, BLUETTI, Anker SOLIX, or EcoFlow changed a watt-hour, inverter rating, weight, or street price, send:

  1. The SKU page on this site (example: Explorer 2000 Plus).
  2. The official product URL you used.
  3. The field that moved (capacity, output, surge, voltage, weight, expandability, street price).
  4. A note if the manufacturer now lists a number we left blank — we leave surge and Voc empty when the live page conflicts or is unclear. We would rather show an em dash than invent a peak watt.
