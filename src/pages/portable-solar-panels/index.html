Silo 4 · Portable solar panels

Portable panels refill a station. They are not a roof.

Fold-out and briefcase panels that plug into a power station. No rails, no microinverters, no net meter.

{{products:panels}} {{compare:panels}}

Who this silo is for

  • You already have (or will buy) a power station and need daytime recharge.
  • You can aim panels at the sun and live with clouds cutting yield in half.
  • You will check Voc against the station’s solar input maximum.

Who should skip it

  • You want set-and-forget production. That is rooftop, out of scope.
  • You have no place to unfold 200 W of panel that is not a theft magnet.
  • Your station’s input is 100 W and you were about to series-wire 800 W of random panels.
