The unit on the website is not the price of whole-house backup.

Shoppers search “whole house generator cost” because the first number they see is equipment-only. Install, gas, and a transfer switch are where quotes actually live.

A realistic all-in for a straightforward air-cooled automatic install is often $7,000–$15,000. Complicated gas or a liquid-cooled “run everything” job climbs from there. If that number ends the conversation, you are not a standby customer yet — look at power stations for home backup or an expandable battery.

When the cost is justified

  • A finished basement that floods without a sump.
  • Well water and a family that cannot haul jugs.
  • Outages measured in days, not hours, in heating season.

When it is a vanity plant

  • One 4-hour outage every three years and a fridge you can live without.
  • You will not exercise the unit. Then you paid for rust.
  • You actually wanted rooftop solar. Different budget, out of scope.
