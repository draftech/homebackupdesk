About

A desk for backup power, not a solar blog.

Home Backup Desk is a niche affiliate site. The working brand is the name on the masthead. The topical cluster is home backup power. That is the whole job.

The owner’s constraint is the architecture: four silos, buyer-intent pages, high-ticket offers, and no sprawl. A new domain with no backlinks should not try to rank for “best solar company” or “Powerwall cost.” Those terms are owned by sites with a decade of links. The working domain we brand toward is homebackupdesk.com — it is not purchased from this repository.

What we publish

  • Whole-house / standby generators — including cost and standby-vs-portable.
  • Home batteries that are expandable and transfer-switch capable, not installer-only ESS quotes.
  • Portable solar generators / portable power stations, with Jackery, Bluetti, and Anker SOLIX as featured offers.
  • Portable solar panels that fold or briefcase into a station. Not roof kits.

What we will not publish

  • Rooftop solar, microinverters, or installer lead-gen.
  • Tesla Powerwall comparisons or Tesla referral links.
  • Guru-tone “energy independence” essays.
  • Invented click IDs, fake “we tested 47 units in our lab” claims, or scraped spec sheets presented as reviews.

Specs in tables are buyer snapshots. Manufacturers change firmware, SKUs, and inverter ratings. Verify the live product page before you spend four figures. Read how we review and the affiliate disclosure.
