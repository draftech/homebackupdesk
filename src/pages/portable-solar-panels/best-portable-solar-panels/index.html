Best-of · Portable panels

The best portable panel is the one your station will accept at noon, not the biggest Amazon tile.

Nameplate watts are lab numbers. Buyers lose money on open-circuit voltage, extra connectors, and 40 lb “portable” briefcases they will not carry. We stay in-family with Jackery, Bluetti, and Anker SOLIX because cable and Voc matching is part of the product.

Who a 200 W folding panel is for

  • You will be home to tilt it a couple of times a day.
  • Your station’s max solar input is at least that wattage at a legal Voc.
  • Weight around 6–10 kg is acceptable.

Who should skip portable panels

  • Outages are 4 hours at night. A panel does nothing. Buy Wh instead.
  • You wanted roof production. Out of scope.
  • You will leave $400 of glass on a street-facing lawn unsupervised.

Expect well under nameplate in winter, haze, or a window. Partial shade on one cell can crater a string. We do not feature random unbranded 400 W blankets that over-Voc a Jackery input. EcoFlow panels exist; they are not featured partners here.
