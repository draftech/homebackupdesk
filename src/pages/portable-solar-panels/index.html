Silo 4 · Portable solar panels

Portable panels refill a station in daylight. They are not a roof, a net meter, or a set-and-forget plant.

Fold-out kits that plug into a power station. You aim them. Clouds cut yield. Open-circuit voltage can kill a charge controller if you ignore the station’s window.

A driveway is the honest workplace for this product. You unfold, you kickstand, you rotate a couple of times, you fold before hail. That is not a rail-and-inverter roof. It is also not a briefcase we can show as a named SKU — we do not have an official briefcase photograph in the catalog, so we describe that format on its own page without inventing a model.

This silo exists because a power station is a tank. Daytime sun is one way to refill it when the wall outlet is dead. Overnight outages do not care how many watts the blanket says. Multi-day grid-down in decent sun is the job. Theft, wind, and Voc mistakes are how the job fails.

The Anker SOLIX PS400 is the 400 W nameplate at 35.3 lb and $699.99 — twice the sticker watts, and a carry you will feel in the wrists. Confirm the station’s solar watt and Voc window before you assume 400 W is a gift.

Who this silo is for

  • You already have, or will buy, a power station and need daytime recharge.
  • You can aim panels and live with clouds cutting yield roughly in half.
  • You will read Voc against the station’s solar input maximum, with margin for cold sun.
  • You have a driveway, yard, or south wall that is not a theft magnet.

Who should skip it

  • You want set-and-forget production. That is rooftop, out of scope.
  • Outages are four hours at night. Buy watt-hours, not glass.
  • Your station’s input is tiny and you were about to series-wire random blankets.
  • You have nowhere to unfold 200–400 W that you can watch.

How to use a driveway array without fooling yourself

Nameplate watts are a lab number at a defined irradiance and temperature. A wet autumn driveway with leaves and a low sun is not that lab. Partial shade on one cell can crater a string. Kickstands blow over. A window-through-glass charge is a demo. Plan on well under nameplate in winter and haze.

  • Check Voc before any series jumper.
  • Fold before hail and before you leave the house.
  • Do not leave four figures of panel as lawn art.
  • Match the cable family to the station you already chose.
  1. Buy the station job first — fridge surge, 120 vs 240 — then size panels to recharge, not the reverse.
  2. Read the solar input window on the station (watts, Voc, sometimes Isc).
  3. Pick a named in-family panel from the cards above.
  4. Weigh the carry. 14–17 lb is a folder. 35.3 lb is a project.

Honest caveats

We do not feature unbranded 400 W blankets that over-Voc a Jackery input. EcoFlow panels exist; they are not featured partners (7-day cookie). We have not invented briefcase SKUs to fill a photo hole. Method: how we review.
