Home backup power only

Pick the backup that matches the outage, not the brochure.

Home Backup Desk is a small, opinionated site for people buying backup power for a house, apartment, or cabin. We stay in one topical cluster: generators, batteries, portable power stations, and the panels that recharge them.

We do not cover rooftop solar arrays, net metering, or Tesla Powerwall. If you need a full automatic standby plant, start in generators. If you need a fridge, CPAP, and Wi-Fi through a 12-hour outage, start with power stations.
