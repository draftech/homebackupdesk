Money page · Home backup

A power station can back up a house’s essentials. It cannot impersonate the grid.

Search demand for “solar generator for home” is real. The honest product is a portable power station on the kitchen floor, maybe feeding an inlet box, not a silent 20 kW plant.

What usually works

  • Refrigerator / freezer, after you confirm surge (see buying guide).
  • Cable modem, ONT, Wi-Fi, phones, laptops.
  • CPAP (often happier on DC than on a cheap inverter).
  • LED lighting and a well pump only if voltage and surge match — many wells are 240 V.

What usually fails

  • Central air or a big heat pump on a 120 V 2 kWh box.
  • Electric dryers, ranges, tankless electric water.
  • Whole-home space heating used as a battery dump.
  • Any load behind a generator interlock you have not labeled and tested.

This page is for you if

  • Outages are hours to a day and you can live without HVAC.
  • You want quiet, indoor-safe backup more than unlimited kW.
  • You will pair panels from silo 4 for longer events.
