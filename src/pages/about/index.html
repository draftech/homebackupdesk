About

A desk for backup power, not a solar blog.

The Home Backup is a niche affiliate site. The working brand is the name on the masthead. The topical cluster is home backup power. That is the whole job.

thehomebackup.com · four silos · no rooftop solar

The constraint is the architecture: four silos, buyer-intent pages, high-ticket offers, and no sprawl. A new domain with no backlinks should not try to rank for “best solar company” or “Powerwall cost.” Those terms are owned by sites with a decade of links. The working domain we brand toward is thehomebackup.com — it is not purchased from this repository. The GitHub repo can stay named for the older working title; the public masthead is The Home Backup.

Shoppers who land here are usually in a hurry for the wrong reason. The lights already flickered, or a storm is two days out, and they want a single SKU that “covers the house.” Most houses are not one SKU. A fridge and a router are a portable power station. A 240 V well is a split-phase battery or a pad generator. A week of ice on the lines is fuel, not a 2 kWh brick. We write the fork in the road first, then the cart.

What we publish

  • Whole-house / standby generators — including cost and standby-vs-portable. Informational. We do not fake a Generac (or similar) affiliate ID.
  • Home batteries that are expandable and transfer-switch capable, not installer-only ESS quotes. The split-phase SKU we name is the Anker SOLIX F3800.
  • Portable solar generators / portable power stations, with Jackery, BLUETTI, and Anker SOLIX as featured offers. Named cores: Explorer 2000 Plus, AC200L, C2000 Gen 2.
  • Portable solar panels that fold or briefcase into a station. Named panels: SolarSaga 200W, SP200L, PS400. Not roof kits.

What we will not publish

  • Rooftop solar, microinverters, or installer lead-gen.
  • Tesla Powerwall comparisons or Tesla referral links.
  • Guru-tone “energy independence” essays.
  • Invented click IDs, fake “we tested 47 units in our lab” claims, or scraped spec sheets presented as reviews.
  • Generated or stand-in product shots for named Jackery, BLUETTI, or Anker SOLIX SKUs. Photos come from the manufacturer’s US product page and are committed in this repo.

How the money works

If you follow a partner link and later buy, we may earn a commission. You still pay the listed price. Featured hops are placeholders until an approved tracking URL is pasted: /go/jackery, /go/bluetti, /go/anker-solix. Full text: affiliate disclosure. Cookie preference: roughly 30 days, which is why EcoFlow is not a featured partner here.

Specs in tables are buyer snapshots dated August 26, 2026. Manufacturers change firmware, SKUs, and inverter ratings. Verify the live product page before you spend four figures. Read how we review. Corrections: contact.

Not electrical advice

Nothing on this site is a permit, a load calculation, or a blessing to backfeed a dryer outlet. Inlet boxes, interlocks, and automatic transfer switches are licensed work in almost every US jurisdiction. Carbon monoxide rules for portable gas generators are not optional.
