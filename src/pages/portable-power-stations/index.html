Silo 3 · Portable power stations

Call them solar generators if you want. They are batteries with inverters.

This silo is the 120 V fridge-and-network buy. Three named SKUs. Four buyer pages. Official manufacturer photographs. Dated specs. No rooftop. No Powerwall.

Updated August 26, 2026 · The Home Backup · thehomebackup.com

A portable power station is a lithium pack, an inverter, and outlets you can see. People search “solar generator.” The solar is a folding panel you aim by hand. The generator part is the inverter. If the utility drops, you plug the refrigerator and the ONT into the case. You do not energize the whole service. You do not backfeed a dryer outlet.

The Home Backup features one class in this aisle: about 2 kWh, 120 V, lithium iron phosphate. Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus (2,042.8 Wh, 3,000 W, 61.5 lb, $899). BLUETTI AC200L (2,048 Wh, 2,400 W, 62.4 lb, expandable to 8,192 Wh, $899). Anker SOLIX C2000 Gen 2 (2,048 Wh, 2,400 W, 4,000 W peak, 41.7 lb, up to 4 kWh, $999.99). Street prices are from the US manufacturer pages on August 26, 2026. Surge on Jackery and BLUETTI is blank because those live pages were unclear or conflicting. If your job is a 240 V well or a split-phase air handler, leave this hub. The Anker 240 V fork is the F3800 in home batteries. If the house itself has to stay alive for days, start with whole-house generators. If you need daytime refill, stay for the station then add portable solar panels.

Who this silo is for

  • You have a 120 V essentials list: fridge, network, lights, maybe CPAP.
  • You can live without HVAC for a typical local outage.
  • You want a quiet, indoor-adjacent pack instead of a gasoline frame.
  • You will test the compressor once before the storm.

Who should skip it

  • Automatic whole-home HVAC — standby generators.
  • 240 V well or dryer — expandable home batteries and the F3800.
  • Roof kits or Tesla Powerwall. Out of scope. About.
