Who the 2 kWh class is for

  • You looked up the fridge’s running watts and will test surge once.
  • You will add portable panels if storms last more than a day.
  • You want a 30-day cookie brand, not a mystery Amazon house label.

Who should skip portable stations

  • Automatic whole-home HVAC — standby generators.
  • A 240 V well on a 120 V explorer — wrong inverter. See the Anker SOLIX F3800.
  • You only camp twice a year and hate weight; buy smaller or rent.

What we skip on purpose

  • EcoFlow as a featured pick. Competitive hardware, weak cookie fit for this research cycle.
  • No-name sub-$200 stations. Below the high-ticket constraint.
  • Tesla Powerwall. Not portable, not in-scope.

We have not personally cycled every SKU through a winter. Rankings are job-based plus documented electrical constraints. Test your house before the storm. Method.
