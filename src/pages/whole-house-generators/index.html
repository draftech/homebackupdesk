Silo 1 · Whole-house generators

Standby generators are a construction project with a motor attached.

A whole-house generator is the right tool when the house itself has to stay alive: furnace or air handler, well pump, sump, freezer, and maybe one HVAC condenser. It is the wrong tool when you only need a fridge and a router.

Who this silo is for

  • Homes with 240 V loads that a 120 V power station cannot feed.
  • Multi-day winter outages where fuel delivery still exists (propane/NG).
  • Medical or accessibility needs that cannot wait on a manual inlet box.

Who should skip it

  • Renters, HOA pads that ban outdoor plants, or anyone without a path to a permit.
  • Shoppers whose real budget is $1–3k all-in — look at power stations.
  • People who actually wanted a battery that charges from a roof. That is out of scope here.
