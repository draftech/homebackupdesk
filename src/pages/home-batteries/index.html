Silo 2 · Home batteries

Home batteries here means expandable backup you can actually buy online.

This silo is not an installer quote for a wall-mounted ESS tied to a roof array. Tesla Powerwall is out of scope. We cover the high-ticket battery systems that sit at the overlap of “portable station” and “partial home backup”: Anker SOLIX F3800-class, Bluetti expandable towers, Jackery HomePower-class kits.

Who this silo is for

  • You want kWh you can grow, often LiFePO4, without a solar sales visit.
  • You can add a transfer switch or inlet and will size 120 V vs 240 V honestly.
  • You prefer silence and no exhaust vs a pad generator.

Who should skip it

  • You need automatic whole-home HVAC for days — start with standby generators.
  • You already have rooftop solar and were told to buy a Powerwall. Different desk.
  • You only need a fridge overnight — a smaller power station is enough.
