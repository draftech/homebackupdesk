Who the F3800 is for

  • You have at least one 240 V load you refuse to give up.
  • You will pay an electrician for a legal interconnect, not a suicide cord.
  • You want to add kWh later instead of guessing year-one capacity.

Who should buy a smaller station instead

  • Apartment, condo, or no place for a 132 lb cabinet on casters.
  • Budget under the high-ticket line after tax — see best power stations.
  • You wanted a roof-tied ESS. Out of scope.

Honest caveats

kWh is finite. A 3.8 kWh pack running a space heater is a short evening. Expandability helps days, not magic. EcoFlow makes competing kits; we are not featuring them while cookie windows are a 7-day risk.
