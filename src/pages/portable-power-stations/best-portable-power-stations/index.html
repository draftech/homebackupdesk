Best-of · Power stations

Best portable power station is a job title, not a trophy.

A 1 kWh class unit can be the right buy for a CPAP and a modem. It is the wrong buy for a refrigerator that surges. We pick classes from Jackery, Bluetti, and Anker SOLIX — the high-ticket programs this site is built to feature.

Quick picks

Fridge-first home backup (~2 kWh class)

Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus-class, Bluetti AC200L-class, Anker SOLIX F2000-class: about 2 kWh, 2000 W+ output, LiFePO4, extra-battery paths. This is the default “keep the fridge and the network up” purchase.

Jackery · Bluetti · Anker SOLIX

Value under the big-box flagship (~1 kWh class)

Jackery 1000-class, Bluetti AC180-class, Anker SOLIX C1000-class. Fine for electronics, lights, and a carefully tested mini-fridge. Prove the compressor start before you trust a full-size fridge overnight.

Who the 2 kWh class is for

  • You measured or looked up the fridge’s running watts and will test surge once.
  • You will add portable panels if storms last more than a day.
  • You want a 30-day cookie brand, not a mystery Amazon house label.

Who should skip portable stations

  • Automatic whole-home HVAC — standby generators.
  • 240 V well pump on a 120 V explorer — wrong inverter.
  • You only camp twice a year and hate weight; buy smaller or rent.

What we skip on purpose

  • EcoFlow as a featured pick. Competitive hardware, weak cookie fit for this research cycle.
  • No-name sub-$200 stations. Below the high-ticket constraint and often below honest surge ratings.
  • Tesla Powerwall. Not portable, not in-scope.

We have not personally cycled every SKU through a winter. Rankings are job-based classes plus documented electrical constraints. Pass-through UPS behavior, UPS-for-furnace boards, and GFCI nuisance trips are real. Test your house before the storm. Method.
