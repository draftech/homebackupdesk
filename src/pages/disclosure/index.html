Cookie windows

Backup-power shoppers research for weeks: watt-hours, transfer switches, whether a fridge will start. We prefer programs with a roughly 30-day cookie so a reader who compares on a Tuesday and buys on a Saturday still attributes. EcoFlow is often cited with a 7-day cookie. That is a poor fit for this niche, so EcoFlow is not a featured partner here. Hardware can still be good. The program is the mismatch.

How rankings relate to money

Featured SKUs are the ones we can photograph from the manufacturer page and describe with dated specs. We do not rank a product higher because the commission is larger. We do skip categories we will not cover (rooftop solar, Tesla Powerwall) and brands we will not feature (short-cookie programs, no-name sub-$200 stations). If two ~2 kWh LiFePO4 stations are in the same job class, we say so and let voltage, weight, and extra-battery maps decide. See Jackery vs BLUETTI vs Anker SOLIX.
