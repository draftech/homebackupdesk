Silo 2 · Home batteries

Home batteries here means expandable backup you can buy online.

Not an installer quote for a wall pack tied to a roof. Tesla Powerwall is out of scope. The overlap of “portable station” and “partial home backup” is the Anker SOLIX F3800, plus expandable 120 V cores from BLUETTI and Jackery.

{{product:anker-solix-f3800}}
{{product:bluetti-ac200l}} {{product:jackery-explorer-2000-plus}}

F3800 is the split-phase SKU. AC200L and Explorer 2000 Plus are 120 V cores that grow with extra batteries — they do not become a well-pump inverter.

Who this silo is for

  • You want kWh you can grow, often LiFePO4 / LFP, without a solar sales visit.
  • You can add a transfer switch or inlet and will size 120 V vs 240 V honestly.
  • You prefer silence and no exhaust vs a pad generator.

Who should skip it

  • You need automatic whole-home HVAC for days — start with standby generators.
  • You already have rooftop solar and were told to buy a Powerwall. Different desk.
  • You only need a fridge overnight — a smaller power station is enough.
