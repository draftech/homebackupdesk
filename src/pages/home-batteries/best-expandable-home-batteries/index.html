If a page ranks “best home batteries” and leads with Tesla, it is not this silo. We compare kits you can grow with extra modules and, where the inverter allows, feed an inlet or transfer switch.

Who an F3800-class kit is for

  • You have at least one 240 V load you refuse to give up (well, some air handlers).
  • You will pay an electrician for a legal interconnect, not a suicide cord.
  • You want to add kWh later instead of guessing year-one capacity.

Who should buy a smaller station instead

  • Apartment, condo, or no outdoor/garage place for a 40 kg brick.
  • Budget under the high-ticket line after tax — see best power stations.
  • You wanted a roof-tied ESS. Out of scope; we will not pivot into Powerwall.

Honest caveats

kWh is finite. A 3–4 kWh pack running a space heater is a two-hour product. Expandability helps days, not magic. Inverter watts still have to start compressors. EcoFlow makes competing kits; we are not featuring them while cookie windows are a 7-day risk for a weeks-long research cycle. Specs move — read the live listing.
