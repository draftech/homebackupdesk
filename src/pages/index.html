The grid went quiet. What still runs?

Home Backup Desk is the lamp-lit worksheet you wanted before the next outage. Four silos. One cluster. No rooftop-solar pitch.

If the house itself has to stay alive, start with a standby plant. If you need a fridge, CPAP, and Wi-Fi through a twelve-hour blackout, start with a power station. Tesla Powerwall and roof arrays are out of scope — by design.

Throw 1 · GEN

Whole-house generators

Automatic standby on propane or natural gas. HVAC, well, ice storm. Install usually costs more than the engine.

Throw 2 · BATT

Home batteries

Expandable kits that can feed an inlet. Anker SOLIX, Bluetti, Jackery. Not a Powerwall. Not a solar installer visit.

Throw 3 · PORT

Portable power stations

Solar generators you can carry. The first honest buy for fridge-plus-essentials. Jackery, Bluetti, Anker SOLIX.

Throw 4 · PV

Portable solar panels

Folding and briefcase panels that plug into a station. No rails. No microinverters. Voc discipline required.
