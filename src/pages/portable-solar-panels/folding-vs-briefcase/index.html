Vs · Panel formats

Folding panels make watts. Briefcases make fewer watts and survive the wind better.

Format is the vs that actually changes a day of charging. Hinges, kickstands, and how fast you give up in a gust are the product.

Format comparison for station charging
Folding / suitcaseBriefcase (hard case)
Watts per tripUsually higher (100–400 W class)Often 60–120 W

Pack shapeSoft-ish folio, easier in a trunkRigid, stackable, heavier for the watts
Home backup roleDefault for Jackery / BLUETTI / Anker refillSupplemental or harsh-site trickle

Buy folding when

  • You are trying to put meaningful Wh back into a 2 kWh station before sunset.
  • You have a driveway or south wall to babysit the array.

Buy briefcase when

  • Wind or camping abuse would destroy a floppy folio.
  • You accept slow recharge in exchange for a lid that aims itself.
